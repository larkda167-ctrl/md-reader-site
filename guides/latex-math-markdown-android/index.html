How to view LaTeX math in markdown on Android

Updated: 2026-10-29

Markdown itself has no math syntax. Instead, many tools let you write LaTeX between dollar signs:

Inline: $E = mc^2$

Block:

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}
$$

In a viewer without math support, you see exactly that: dollar signs, backslashes and braces. That's a problem for lecture notes, research summaries and AI answers, which are full of formulas.

Where you'll meet LaTeX in markdown

  • AI answers. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini write formulas in LaTeX. When you copy an answer, you get the source.
  • Study notes written in Obsidian, Notion exports or Jupyter-style tools.
  • Technical READMEs and documentation.

Supported syntax

KaTeX covers most of what you'll see in notes and AI answers:

  • Fractions, roots, sums and integrals: \frac, \sqrt, \sum, \int
  • Greek letters and symbols: \alpha, \infty, \leq
  • Matrices: \begin{pmatrix} ... \end{pmatrix}
  • Aligned equations: \begin{aligned} ... \end{aligned}

If a formula has a typo, the rest of the document still renders, and the broken formula is shown so you can find it.

Tips

  • Long formulas scroll sideways instead of overflowing the screen.
  • Dark mode works with formulas too. See comfortable reading settings.
  • Diagrams often appear next to math in technical answers. MD Reader draws those as well. See Mermaid diagrams on Android.
