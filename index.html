What it does

  • Open .md from anywhere

    Tap a file in Files, Downloads, Drive or an email, or share text to the app.

  • Made for AI answers

    Paste a long answer from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and read it as a real document.

  • Edit without symbols

    Fix a typo or add a line on the formatted page. You never have to type ## or **.

  • Checklists you can tap

    Tick a box and it's saved straight back to the file.

  • Math and diagrams

    LaTeX formulas and Mermaid diagrams render on your phone, even offline.

  • Phones, tablets, foldables

    Layouts, five themes and adjustable text for comfortable reading.

Private by design

MD Reader doesn't request internet access. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your files never leave your device.
